Arrive / Flowbird · Work

Ticket Twister

Tickets were jamming as they came out of the printer in a retail ticket kiosk. I designed a guide that turns each ticket cleanly into the collection tray, proved it with a 3D-printed prototype on a test rig, and used the test results to justify making it as a stainless steel sheet-metal part.

59jams without the twister
1jam with the printed prototype
~1,500tickets per test run

What I did

  • Built a test bench with a camera that recorded every ticket, so each jam was logged and photographed automatically.
  • Ran a baseline test without the twister: 59 jams across three batches of 500 tickets, far over the 6-jam pass limit.
  • 3D-printed the Ticket Twister and repeated the test: just 1 jam in 1,507 tickets, a clear pass.
  • Used that evidence to move from the printed prototype to a manufactured part, and drew it up in stainless steel 304 sheet metal with a flat pattern, bend angles, tolerances and a burr-free ticket contact surface, ready for supplier quotes.

For animatronics: prototype cheaply, prove it with data, then commit to production material. The same approach fits any mechanism that has to move reliably thousands of times a day.

AI Camera Housing

A compact enclosure for an AI camera that watches tickets coming out of the printer on the test bench, so the team can see how each ticket behaves as it leaves the machine.

What I did

  • Designed a two-part enclosure around the camera module and main board, with threaded inserts for a lid that can be removed again and again.
  • Mounted the camera on adjustable standoffs so the lens sits flush with the front face.
  • Routed the ribbon cable inside the case and embossed the label straight into the print.

Arrive / Flowbird · Work

Fan Controller

A small control system that switches a cooling fan inside a kiosk. It uses an Arduino microcontroller and a relay, each housed in its own 3D-printed enclosure and mounted inside the cabinet.

What I did

  • Wired the Arduino and the sensing circuit, and soldered the relay onto stripboard.
  • Designed and printed both enclosures, using brass heat-set inserts so they can be opened and closed many times.
  • Mounted the units in the cabinet with tidy cable entry and strain relief.

Bournemouth University · Dissertation

Tidal Micro Power Generator

My final-year dissertation, graded 73%: "Tidal Elevation for Micro Power Generation". I designed, modelled and built a prototype that turns the rise and fall of the tide into small amounts of electrical power for off-grid use.

What I did

  • Designed the mechanism to convert vertical tidal movement into rotation, driving a generator through a pulley.
  • Built the prototype on an aluminium bracket strapped to a marina pile, with a simple LED load circuit.
  • Tested it in place and measured the output with a multimeter (2.43 V in the photo).
